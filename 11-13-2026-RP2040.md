# Abandoning big dreams for my microcontroller

When gifted my Raspberry Pi Pico W (RP2040), my initial plan was to build with it an automatic irrigation system. I recall speaking to a
bio-engineering professor who shared that the hardware was outdated and that he prefers to work with ESP32s. That was 
about 4 years ago. 

Today, I spoke with a professor of computer networking about my goals to run a small home lab with the RP2040. I was 
advised to ditch the hardware and build something industry-grade with Docker, vibe code, and couple hours of time. I could instead 
dedicate the RP2040 to running Pi-hole to block ads. I find this hilarious and sad. 

My next goals for my RP2040 are to:

- [ ] run `print(hello world)`
- [ ] turn the lights on and off
- [ ] write a program to connect to home wifi
- [ ] learn how to remotely access the microcontroller while it is hooked to an external battery
