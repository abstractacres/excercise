End of Fall 2025, winter, I rushed to submit my analysis of AIS signal gap data collected by the Global Fishing Watch to earn 45/75 points.    
  
The goal of Global Fishing Watch organization is to increase the transparency about human activity in our shared ocean. I used their gfwr R package to access their “Events API”. AIS (Automatic Identification System) is a continuous radio transmission broadcasting a ship’s 🚢 position, speed, course, and identity to other vessels and shore stations to prevent collisions. When and where a ship stops and start broadcasting their AIS signal may help lead to insight about illicit activities such as illegal fishing and modern slavery.
