I picked up the second edition of *Modern Elementary Statistics* by John E. Freund from a stack on free books on campus sometime last semester. I took a Statistics with R course after taking Biostatistics course (also using R), and found that I enjoy reviewing the concepts more than once. What a concept. I'll be retaking Statistics with R Spring 2027 and have chosen to focus on tutoring statistics as a volunteer with Schoolhouse.world. With Freund, I anticipate the fun of encountering truths in a book published in 1950s relevant to today. Here's a quick list of quotes and ideas to note from the 10 page introduction:

  * Statistics "constitutes the science of decision-making in the face of uncertainty"
  * The line between descriptive and inductive statistics (or statistical inference) has become less pronounced as data collection, analysis, and presentation is increasing accomplished for the purpose of decision-making
  * Freund synonymized the word "decision" with "generalization"
  * How data is collected and experiments are designed determines whether it is even possible to arrive to valid results
